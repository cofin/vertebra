## Objective

Add a centralized `MsgpackCodec` (`encode_msgpack`, `decode_msgpack`) to `vertebra.utils.serializers`, remove unreachable stdlib `json.dumps`/`json.loads` fallbacks and `global` statements from `src/py/vertebra/utils/serializers/_json.py`, and route all direct `msgspec.msgpack` call sites through `vertebra.utils.serializers`.

## Context

- `src/py/vertebra/utils/serializers/_json.py:60-67` and `:78-85` check `if self._encoder is not None:` and fall back to stdlib `json.dumps`/`json.loads`, which are unreachable because `msgspec` is a required core dependency (`pyproject.toml:34`, `FW-27`).
- `src/py/vertebra/utils/serializers/_json.py:100-106` and `:147-162` use `global _default_serializer` and `global _uuid_utils_type` (`PLW0603`, `FW-27`, `FW-48`).
- Direct `msgspec.msgpack.encode`/`decode`/`Encoder` calls are scattered across `src/py/vertebra/framework/live/__init__.py:126,131`, `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlspec/storage.py:152,177,186`, `src/py/vertebra/extensions/grpc/_decorator.py:267,269,276,279,287,294,313`, and `src/py/vertebra/extensions/alchemy/broker.py:327` (`FW-27`).
- Subsequent tasks that further refactor those call-site files (`5.6` for `framework/live`, `6.1` for `extensions/alchemy/broker.py`, `6.2` for `extensions/sqlspec/storage.py`, and `6.7` for `extensions/grpc/_decorator.py`) declare `depends_on: ["5.3"]` and preserve the `vertebra.utils.serializers` msgpack imports.

## Steps

1. **Remove stdlib `json` fallbacks and `global` state in `src/py/vertebra/utils/serializers/_json.py`**:
   - Remove `import json` and the `if self._encoder is not None:` / `if self._decoder is not None:` branches (`:10,48-85`), initializing `self._encoder = msgspec.json.Encoder(enc_hook=_enc_hook)` and `self._decoder = msgspec.json.Decoder(dec_hook=_dec_hook)` unconditionally in `JSONSerializer.__init__`.
   - Replace `_default_serializer` and `global _default_serializer` (`:97-106`) with a class attribute `JSONSerializer._default: ClassVar[JSONSerializer | None] = None` and `JSONSerializer.default() -> JSONSerializer` (keeping `get_default_serializer()` as a thin caller of `JSONSerializer.default()`).
   - Replace `_uuid_utils_type` and `global _uuid_utils_type` (`:144-162`) with a class attribute on a private `_UuidTypeCache` holder.
2. **Create `src/py/vertebra/utils/serializers/_msgpack.py` and re-export from `src/py/vertebra/utils/serializers/__init__.py`**:
   - Implement `MsgpackCodec` in `src/py/vertebra/utils/serializers/_msgpack.py` with a shared `msgspec.msgpack.Encoder(enc_hook=_enc_hook)`, a `msgspec.msgpack.Decoder(dec_hook=_dec_hook)` for untyped payloads, and a per-type cache `_typed_decoders: dict[Any, msgspec.msgpack.Decoder[Any]]` for typed `decode_msgpack(raw, type=...)` calls.
   - Expose `MsgpackCodec`, `encode_msgpack(value: Any) -> bytes`, and `decode_msgpack(raw: bytes | bytearray | memoryview, *, type: Any = Any) -> Any` in `_msgpack.py` and re-export them in `src/py/vertebra/utils/serializers/__init__.py`.
3. **Replace direct `msgspec.msgpack` calls across `src/py/vertebra/`**:
   - `src/py/vertebra/framework/live/__init__.py:126,131`: replace `msgspec.msgpack.encode` and `msgspec.msgpack.decode` with `encode_msgpack` and `decode_msgpack` from `vertebra.utils.serializers`.
   - `src/py/vertebra/extensions/alchemy/broker.py:327`: replace `_MSGPACK_ENCODER = msgspec.msgpack.Encoder()` with `encode_msgpack` from `vertebra.utils.serializers`.
   - `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlspec/storage.py:152,177,186`: replace `msgspec.msgpack.encode` and `msgspec.msgpack.decode` with `encode_msgpack` and `decode_msgpack`.
   - `src/py/vertebra/extensions/grpc/_decorator.py:267,269,276,279,287,294,313`: replace `msgspec.msgpack.encode` and `msgspec.msgpack.decode(..., type=...)` with `encode_msgpack` and `decode_msgpack(..., type=...)`.
4. **Extend unit tests in `src/py/tests/unit/test_utils_json.py`**:
   - Add round-trip tests for `MsgpackCodec`, `encode_msgpack`, and `decode_msgpack` covering `msgspec.Struct`, typed `type=MyStruct` decoding, `UUID`/`datetime`/`Path` hooks, and `memoryview` inputs.
