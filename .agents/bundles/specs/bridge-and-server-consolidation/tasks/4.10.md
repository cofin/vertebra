# 4.10 Rust-backed telemetry and wired platform controls

## Objective

Fix the UTF-8 corruption, `JOIN (` false-positive rewrite, and missing `VALUES` sanitization in `src/rs/vertebra-core/src/telemetry.rs:100-133` (`CORE-34`), bind `AsyncLogCore`, `W3cTraceContext`, and `sanitize_sql_statement` in `src/rs/vertebra-py/src/telemetry.rs` per Decision D4, and back `vertebra.telemetry.{logger,otel,db_propagation}` with the native Rust implementations (`PYB-55`, `PYB-56`, `CORE-13`). Cap `GenAiRecorder._samples` with a bounded `deque(maxlen=...)` and `drain()` (`PYB-57`), compute `ZPagesRegistry` uptime from `time.monotonic()` (`PYB-54`), wire `CgroupQuota`, `LameDuckController`, `ZPagesRegistry`, and `ShmAllocationPolicy` into `worker.py` and `_supervisor.py` (`PYB-53`), and delete the empty placeholder packages `src/py/vertebra/server/_ipc` and `src/py/vertebra/server/_js` (`PYB-59`).

## Context

- **`CORE-34` (`src/rs/vertebra-core/src/telemetry.rs:100-133`):** `sanitize_sql_statement` iterates `bytes[i] as char` (corrupting multi-byte UTF-8 characters into Latin-1 code points), checks `bytes[i..i + 3].eq_ignore_ascii_case(b"IN ")` without a leading word-boundary check (turning `JOIN (SELECT id FROM t)` into `JOIN (?)`), and documents `VALUES (...)` collapsing without implementing it.
- **`CORE-13`, `PYB-55`, `PYB-56` (`src/rs/vertebra-core/src/logger.rs:1-246`, `telemetry.rs:1-156`, `src/py/vertebra/telemetry/logger.py:116-207`, `otel.py:20-88`, `db_propagation.py:73-77`):** Under roadmap Decision D4, Rust `AsyncLogCore`, `W3cTraceContext`, and `sanitize_sql_statement` are the single implementation and Python `LogSink`, `TraceContext`, and `SqlTracePropagator.sanitize_sql` bind to them via `vertebra-py` (giving `LogSink` real `overflow_policy="block"` and thread-safe lock-free/condvar behavior on 3.14t).
- **`PYB-57` (`src/py/vertebra/telemetry/otel.py:139-168`):** `GenAiRecorder._samples` is an unbounded `list` with no `max_samples` cap and no `drain()` method.
- **`PYB-53` & `PYB-54` (`src/py/vertebra/server/platform.py:22-156`, `src/py/vertebra/server/_shm_hardening.py:13-67`):** `CgroupQuota`, `LameDuckController`, `ZPagesRegistry`, and `ShmAllocationPolicy` are only referenced by unit tests, and `platform.py:111,148` computes `uptime_seconds` from `time.time()` (which jumps under wall-clock NTP adjustments) instead of `time.monotonic()`.
- **`PYB-59` (`src/py/vertebra/server/_ipc/__init__.py`, `src/py/vertebra/server/_js/__init__.py`):** Empty namespace packages with zero importers.
- **Symbol overlap note (depends on 4.9):** Adds `telemetry.rs` to `src/rs/vertebra-py/src/`, updates `lib.rs` and `_vertebra.pyi`, and wires platform controls into `src/py/vertebra/server/worker.py` (created in Task 4.7).
- **Strategy justification:** `regression_tdd` is required because `sanitize_sql_statement` corrupts UTF-8 identifiers and `JOIN (` subqueries, `LogSink` ignores `overflow_policy="block"`, and `GenAiRecorder` grows without bound.
