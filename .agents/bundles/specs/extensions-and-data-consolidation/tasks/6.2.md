## Objective

Deduplicate `SQLSpecSyncConfig`/`SQLSpecAsyncConfig` and `SyncDatabaseConfig`/`AsyncDatabaseConfig` in `extensions/sqlspec/plugin.py`, delete redundant `SQLSpecConfig.wrap` and `SQLSpecPlugin.wrap_config` wrappers, offload synchronous driver teardown in `SQLSpecPlugin._close_target` via `SyncPool`, delegate `SqlSpecStorageBridge` to `StorageRegistry`, remove duplicate Oracle pool registries in `extensions/sqlspec/oracle.py` in favor of `vertebra.db.oracle`, and wire `LitestarFastPathBypass` in `LitestarPlugin`.

## Context

- `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlspec/plugin.py:39-226` duplicates ~140 lines across `SQLSpecSyncConfig` (`:39-109`) and `SQLSpecAsyncConfig` (`:112-182`) plus two more wrapper classes `SyncDatabaseConfig` (`:185-204`) and `AsyncDatabaseConfig` (`:207-226`, `FW-22`, `FW-24`).
- `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlspec/plugin.py:237-240` (`SQLSpecConfig.wrap`) and `:382-385` (`SQLSpecPlugin.wrap_config`) are 1-line constructor aliases (`FW-21`), and `SQLSpecPlugin._close_target` (`:388-402`) calls synchronous `close()` / `dispose()` directly on the asyncio event loop (`FW-45`) and uses `except Exception: pass` (`S110`, `BLE001`, `FW-48`).
- `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlspec/storage.py:36-119` (`SqlSpecStorageBridge`) reimplements alias registration, URI resolution, and JSON/bytes helpers already provided by `StorageRegistry` (`src/py/vertebra/storage/registry.py`, `FW-20`).
- `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlspec/oracle.py:31-152` (`derive_isolated_oracle_config`, `SqlSpecOraclePoolEntry`, `SqlSpecOraclePoolRegistry`) duplicates `OracleConfig.derive_pool` (`src/py/vertebra/db/oracle/config.py:84-106`) and `OraclePoolRegistry` (`src/py/vertebra/db/oracle/driver.py:141-183`), and `OracleVectorConverter.to_oracle_vector` (`:166-176`) duplicates `SyncOracleDriver.encode_vector` (`FW-22`, `FW-46`).
- `src/py/vertebra/extensions/litestar/plugin.py:17` imports `Route` and `Router` from `vertebra.framework` without using them, and `src/py/vertebra/extensions/litestar/fastpath.py:21-48` (`LitestarFastPathBypass`) is never wired into `LitestarPlugin.on_app_init` (`FW-40`).

## Steps

1. **Consolidate `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlspec/plugin.py` (`FW-21`, `FW-22`, `FW-24`, `FW-45`, `FW-48`)**:
   - Extract `_BaseSQLSpecConfig(msgspec.Struct, frozen=True, gc=False, kw_only=True)` holding shared fields (`bind_key`, `dsn`, `min_size`, `max_size`, `statement_cache_size`, `enable_arrow`, `driver_features`), validation in `__post_init__`, and `effective_pool_config`.
   - Have `SQLSpecSyncConfig[ConnectionT, PoolT, DriverT]` and `SQLSpecAsyncConfig[ConnectionT, PoolT, DriverT]` inherit from `_BaseSQLSpecConfig`, and alias `SyncDatabaseConfig = SQLSpecSyncConfig` and `AsyncDatabaseConfig = SQLSpecAsyncConfig` (retaining generic type parameter subscripting).
   - Delete `SQLSpecConfig.wrap` (`:237-240`) and `SQLSpecPlugin.wrap_config` (`:382-385`).
   - In `SQLSpecPlugin._close_target` (`:388-402`), when `close_fn` is synchronous (`not inspect.iscoroutinefunction(close_fn)`), execute `await SyncPool.default().submit_async(close_fn)` and log errors with `logger.debug(..., exc_info=True)` instead of `except Exception: pass`.
2. **Delegate `SqlSpecStorageBridge` to `StorageRegistry` in `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlspec/storage.py` (`FW-20`)**:
   - Refactor `SqlSpecStorageBridge` to wrap a `StorageRegistry` instance (defaulting to `get_default_storage_registry()`), delegating `register_alias`, `resolve_store`, `put_bytes`, `get_bytes`, `put_json`, and `get_json` directly to `StorageRegistry` and `ObjectStore` while keeping `ArrowFlightSerializer` (using `encode_msgpack`/`decode_msgpack` from `vertebra.utils.serializers`).
3. **Retain `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlspec/oracle.py` as a clean submodule re-exported by `__init__.py` (`FW-22`, `FW-46`)**:
   - Keep `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlspec/oracle.py` as a clean submodule re-exported by `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlspec/__init__.py` so `vertebra-native-db-drivers` Task `2.2` (`OracleHybridSearch` / `OracleVectorStore`) extends `oracle.py` cleanly.
   - Re-export `OracleConfig` and `OraclePoolRegistry` from `vertebra.db.oracle` in place of `SqlSpecOraclePoolRegistry` and `derive_isolated_oracle_config`, delegate `OracleVectorConverter.to_oracle_vector` directly to `SyncOracleDriver.encode_vector`, and update `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlspec/__init__.py` exports (sorting `__all__` per `RUF022`).
4. **Clean up `LitestarPlugin` and wire `LitestarFastPathBypass` (`FW-40`)**:
   - In `src/py/vertebra/extensions/litestar/plugin.py:17`, remove the unused `Route` and `Router` imports.
   - In `LitestarPlugin.on_app_init`, when `self._config.enable_fastpath_bypass` is `True`, instantiate `LitestarFastPathBypass(self._router_table)` from `vertebra.extensions.litestar.fastpath` and store it on `app_config.state["vertebra_fastpath_bypass"]`.
5. **Update unit tests in `src/py/tests/unit/extensions/test_data_storage_integrations.py`**:
   - Update tests to use `SQLSpecConfig(...)` constructor directly (instead of `.wrap()`), verify `SQLSpecPlugin.shutdown()` offloads sync driver close to `SyncPool`, and verify `OracleVectorConverter` and `OraclePoolRegistry` integration.
