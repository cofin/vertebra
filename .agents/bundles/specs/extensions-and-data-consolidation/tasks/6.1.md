## Objective

Fix `ShmSessionBroker` lock safety and `O(N)` waiter removal in `extensions/alchemy/broker.py`, compile SQLAlchemy `ClauseElement` objects in `StatementCache`, deduplicate `SyncSessionConfig`/`AsyncSessionConfig` and execute real SQLAlchemy queries in `DatabaseService` (`extensions/alchemy/plugin.py`), delegate `StoredObject` directly to `ObjectStore` (`extensions/alchemy/storage.py`), consolidate `types.py` and `optimizations.py` into `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlalchemy/__init__.py` (or `dialects.py`), and keep `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlalchemy/dialects.py` and `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlalchemy/alembic.py` as clean submodules re-exported by `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlalchemy/__init__.py` so `vertebra-native-db-drivers` tasks (`1.3`, `2.1`, `2.3`) can extend `dialects.py` and `alembic.py` cleanly without file-deletion conflicts.

## Context

- `src/py/vertebra/extensions/alchemy/broker.py:165,236,243-253` stores `self._waiters: list[asyncio.Future[None]]`, calls `self._waiters.remove(waiter)` in `O(N)` on timeout (`:236`), and mutates `self._in_use` and `self._waiters` inside `release_async()` (`:243-253`) without acquiring `self._lock`, racing with synchronous `acquire()`/`release()` (`:184-216`, `FW-42`).
- `src/py/vertebra/extensions/alchemy/broker.py:330-355` (`StatementCache.compile_and_encode`) converts `statement` with `str(statement)` without compiling SQLAlchemy `ClauseElement` objects or extracting bind parameter positions (`FW-05`).
- `src/py/vertebra/extensions/alchemy/plugin.py:30-123` duplicates ~95 lines across `SyncSessionConfig` and `AsyncSessionConfig` (`FW-23`). In the same file, `DatabaseService` (`:256-339`) runs two separate queries in `list_and_count` (`:256-280`, `FW-43`) and its `count()` method (`:333-339`) calls `len(await self.list(*filters))` instead of `SELECT count(*)` when backed by a SQLAlchemy session (`FW-06`).
- `src/py/vertebra/extensions/alchemy/storage.py:57-128` uses `getattr(store, ...)` reflection instead of calling `ObjectStore` methods directly (`FW-39`) and reimplements URL query signature verification in `StoredObject.verify_presigned_url` (`:109-128`) duplicating `ObjectStore.verify_presigned_url` (`FW-20`).
- `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlalchemy/{dialects,types,optimizations,alembic}.py` are 4 small files with an inert `class TypeDecorator` stub (`types.py:13-22`), unconnected `PlainTupleAdapter` (`optimizations.py:42-77`), and `AlembicRunner` (`alembic.py:55-96`, `FW-44`).

## Steps

1. **Write failing regression tests in `test_drop_in_names_and_generics.py` and `test_sqlalchemy_and_sqlspec_vtb.py`**:
   - Add a concurrency test in `test_drop_in_names_and_generics.py` exercising interleaved `ShmSessionBroker.acquire()`, `release()`, `acquire_async()`, timeout cancellation, and `release_async()` across threads and tasks, verifying `in_use` never underflows and cancelled waiters are skipped in `O(1)`.
   - Add tests verifying `StatementCache.compile_and_encode` extracts compiled SQL and positional bind parameter names from a SQLAlchemy Core/ORM selectable or `.compile()`-capable statement, and verifying `DatabaseService.count()` and `list_and_count()` execute SQL count/window expressions rather than materializing full table lists.
2. **Fix `ShmSessionBroker` and `StatementCache` in `src/py/vertebra/extensions/alchemy/broker.py` (`FW-05`, `FW-42`, `FW-48`)**:
   - Replace `self._waiters: list[asyncio.Future[None]]` with `collections.deque[asyncio.Future[None]]`. On timeout in `acquire_async()`, call `waiter.cancel()` without `self._waiters.remove(waiter)`; in both `release()` and `release_async()`, hold `with self._lock:` while decrementing `self._in_use` and popping from `self._waiters` until an active (`not waiter.done()`) future is woken or `_waiters` is empty.
   - In `StatementCache.compile_and_encode`, check `compile_fn = getattr(statement, "compile", None)`; when callable, invoke `compiled = compile_fn()` and extract `sql_text = str(compiled)` and `param_order = tuple( getattr(compiled, "positiontup", None) or tuple(getattr(compiled, "params", {}).keys()) )`.
   - Sort `__all__` (`RUF022`) and narrow any broad `except Exception` catches (`BLE001`).
3. **Deduplicate session configs and upgrade `DatabaseService` in `src/py/vertebra/extensions/alchemy/plugin.py` (`FW-06`, `FW-23`, `FW-43`, `FW-48`)**:
   - Extract `_BaseSessionConfig(msgspec.Struct, frozen=True, gc=False, kw_only=True)` holding `bind_key`, `dsn`, `pool_size`, `max_overflow`, `pool_timeout`, `pool_recycle`, `pool_pre_ping`, `echo`, `execution_options`, `__post_init__`, and `effective_engine_config`, with `SyncSessionConfig` and `AsyncSessionConfig` inheriting from `_BaseSessionConfig`.
   - In `DatabaseService`, when `self._session` exposes SQLAlchemy `.execute` and `self.model_type` is mapped, build `sqlalchemy.select(sqlalchemy.func.count()).select_from(self.model_type)` in `count()` and `sqlalchemy.select(self.model_type, sqlalchemy.func.count().over().label("_total_count"))` in `list_and_count()`.
   - Replace silent `except Exception: pass` during teardown (`:228-235`) with `logger.debug("Ignoring error closing SQLAlchemy session/engine", exc_info=True)` (`S110`, `BLE001`).
4. **Simplify `StoredObject` in `src/py/vertebra/extensions/alchemy/storage.py` (`FW-20`, `FW-39`)**:
   - Call `store.put`, `store.get`, `store.delete`, `store.put_async`, `store.get_async`, `store.delete_async`, `store.presign_get`, `store.presign_put`, and `store.verify_presigned_url` directly on the resolved `ObjectStore` from `StorageRegistry`, deleting the custom query-string HMAC verifier in `StoredObject.verify_presigned_url` (`:109-128`).
5. **Consolidate `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlalchemy/__init__.py` while preserving `dialects.py` and `alembic.py` (`FW-44`)**:
   - Consolidate `types.py` and `optimizations.py` into `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlalchemy/__init__.py` or `dialects.py` (deleting `types.py` and `optimizations.py`).
   - Keep `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlalchemy/dialects.py` and `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlalchemy/alembic.py` as clean submodules re-exported by `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlalchemy/__init__.py` so downstream `vertebra-native-db-drivers` tasks (`1.3`, `2.1`, `2.3`) can extend `dialects.py` and `alembic.py` cleanly without file-deletion conflicts.
   - Have `attach_serverless_disconnect_guard(engine)` register a `sqlalchemy.event.listen(engine, "engine_connect", ...)` listener when `sqlalchemy.event` is importable, and have `AlembicRunner.upgrade`/`downgrade` invoke `alembic.command.upgrade`/`downgrade` when `alembic` is installed.
