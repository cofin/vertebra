# 3.9 Realtime topic hub

## Objective

Consolidate `sse.rs`, `pubsub.rs`, `ws_mount.rs`, `grpc.rs`, and `hmr.rs` under `src/rs/vertebra-http/src/realtime/{mod,topic_hub,sse,pubsub,ws,grpc,hmr}.rs`: unify `SseHub` and `PubSubBroker` onto a single `realtime::TopicHub` and rename `ShmBroadcastRing` to `BroadcastRing` (`HTTP-35`), eliminate per-event `Vec<&str>` line splitting and `String` header sanitization in `sse.rs` (`HTTP-44`), build materialized gRPC responses without spawning a Tokio task (`HTTP-36`), and make `hmr.rs` reuse `realtime::ws::build_handshake_response` and `WsConnectionRegistry` (`HTTP-45`).

## Context

- **`HTTP-35` (`src/rs/vertebra-http/src/pubsub.rs:100-168,347-409`, `src/rs/vertebra-http/src/sse.rs:259-325`):** Publishing an event through `PubSubBroker` copies `topic.to_owned()`, clones the envelope twice, broadcasts on three channels, converts payloads with `from_utf8_lossy` into an `SseEvent`, and re-encodes into a second replay ring in `SseHub`. `ShmBroadcastRing` is a heap `Vec<Mutex<Vec<u8>>>` (not shared memory) that clones every frame on `read_since`.
- **`HTTP-44` (`src/rs/vertebra-http/src/sse.rs:146-148,162-193,223-242`):** `encode_sse_event` is a one-line wrapper around `SseEvent::encode`, `push` and `push_encoded` duplicate eviction logic, `split_whatwg_lines` allocates a `Vec<&str>` per encode, and `sanitize_header_field` allocates a `String` even when no CR/LF characters are present.
- **`HTTP-36` (`src/rs/vertebra-http/src/grpc.rs:337-371`):** `build_grpc_stream_response` spawns a Tokio task and an `mpsc` channel just to iterate a `Vec<GrpcFrame>` that is already in memory.
- **`HTTP-45` (`src/rs/vertebra-http/src/hmr.rs:153-184`, `src/rs/vertebra-http/src/ws_mount.rs:225-290`):** `hmr.rs` manually constructs its own `101 Switching Protocols` response and SHA-1 accept key instead of calling `ws_mount::build_handshake_response`, and never registers HMR connections in `WsConnectionRegistry`.
- **Symbol overlap note (depends on 3.8):** Moves `sse.rs`, `http3.rs`, and `grpc.rs` (whose body wrappers were replaced in 3.1) into `realtime/`, and wires `realtime::grpc::is_grpc_request` and `realtime::hmr` into `ingress/{mod,pipeline}.rs`.
- **Strategy justification:** `characterization` is used because SSE, pub/sub, WebSocket, gRPC, and HMR wire behavior are preserved while consolidating duplicate topic/replay stores and handshakes, paired with a new `realtime/publish_1k_subscribers` Criterion benchmark.

## Steps

- [x] 1. Create `src/rs/vertebra-http/src/realtime/topic_hub.rs` (`HTTP-35`):
  - Define `TopicHub` holding per-topic state `{ seq: AtomicU64, replay: RwLock<VecDeque<(u64, Bytes)>>, tx: broadcast::Sender<PublishedItem> }` where `PublishedItem` stores `seq: u64`, `payload: Bytes`, and a lazily or once-encoded `sse_frame: Bytes` so both raw pub/sub subscribers and SSE subscribers share one replay ring and one broadcast fan-out.
  - Rename `ShmBroadcastRing` (`pubsub.rs:347-409`) to `BroadcastRing` storing `Bytes` slots so `read_since` clones `Bytes` (refcount bump) instead of `Vec<u8>`.
- [x] 2. Move `src/rs/vertebra-http/src/sse.rs` to `src/rs/vertebra-http/src/realtime/sse.rs` and `src/rs/vertebra-http/src/pubsub.rs` to `src/rs/vertebra-http/src/realtime/pubsub.rs` (deleting root `sse.rs` and `pubsub.rs`, `HTTP-35`, `HTTP-44`):
  - Back both `SseHub` and `PubSubBroker` with `Arc<TopicHub>`.
  - Delete `encode_sse_event` wrapper (`sse.rs:146-148`), deduplicate `push` into `push_encoded`, change `split_whatwg_lines(input: &str) -> impl Iterator<Item = &str>` to return a zero-allocation iterator, and change `sanitize_header_field(input: &str) -> Cow<'_, str>` to borrow `input` when no `\r` or `\n` is present.
- [x] 3. Move `src/rs/vertebra-http/src/grpc.rs` to `src/rs/vertebra-http/src/realtime/grpc.rs` (deleting root `grpc.rs`, `HTTP-36`):
  - In `build_grpc_stream_response`, when no trailers are present, concatenate LPM-encoded frames into a single `Bytes` and return `body::full_body(buf)`. When trailers are present, send the encoded data frame and `Frame::trailers(trailers)` into a 2-slot channel without spawning a background task, or wire `is_grpc_request` into `ingress::Pipeline`.
- [x] 4. Move `src/rs/vertebra-http/src/ws_mount.rs` to `src/rs/vertebra-http/src/realtime/ws.rs` and `src/rs/vertebra-http/src/hmr.rs` to `src/rs/vertebra-http/src/realtime/hmr.rs` (deleting root `ws_mount.rs` and `hmr.rs`, `HTTP-45`):
  - In `realtime/hmr.rs`, replace the custom 101 response builder (`hmr.rs:153-184`) and `derive_accept_key` with `realtime::ws::build_handshake_response`, and register active HMR sockets in `WsConnectionRegistry` so graceful shutdown `close_all` closes HMR clients too.
- [x] 5. Create `src/rs/vertebra-http/src/realtime/mod.rs` exporting `topic_hub`, `sse`, `pubsub`, `ws`, `grpc`, and `hmr`; update `src/rs/vertebra-http/src/lib.rs`, `src/rs/vertebra-http/src/ingress/mod.rs`, `src/rs/vertebra-http/src/ingress/pipeline.rs`, and the five realtime test files; and add the `realtime/publish_1k_subscribers` benchmark group in `src/rs/vertebra-http/benches/server_throughput.rs`.
- [x] 6. Commit: `refactor(http): consolidate SSE, pubsub, WebSocket, gRPC, and HMR under realtime::TopicHub` (signed), staging exactly the paths in `files`.
