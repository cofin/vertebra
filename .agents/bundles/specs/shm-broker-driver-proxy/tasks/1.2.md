# Task 1.2: Implement `ProxySyncDriver` and `ProxyAsyncDriver` in `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlspec/proxy.py` and wire `ProxySyncConfig[SyncConfigT]`, `ProxyAsyncConfig[AsyncConfigT]`, and `SQLSpecPlugin`

## Objective

Create `ProxySyncDriver` and `ProxyAsyncDriver` in `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlspec/proxy.py` and wire `ProxySyncConfig[SyncConfigT]` and `ProxyAsyncConfig[AsyncConfigT]` in `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlspec/plugin.py:272-453` so `provide_session()` and `provide_connection()` acquire a `ConnectionLease` from `self.broker`, consult `get_or_compile_statement` on the shared-memory statement cache, and route execution through `SqlSpecProvider` with zero-copy Arrow IPC deserialization.

## Context

- `src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlspec/plugin.py:272-453`: `ProxySyncConfig` and `ProxyAsyncConfig` currently store `self.broker` on `__slots__ = ("broker", "config")` (lines 277, 368), but `provide_session()` (lines 329-332, 420-423) and `provide_connection()` (lines 334-337, 425-428) ignore `self.broker` and call `self.config.provide_session()` directly.
- `SQLSpecPlugin` (`src/py/vertebra/extensions/sqlspec/plugin.py:485-650`) accepts `broker: BrokerPlugin | None = None` and installs `broker_transport` in `install_state`.

## Steps
